When a new card enters the DOM (at the 'Last' position), it had no initial prop. Framer Motion defaults to reasonable values (like scale: 1), but our 'dormant' state is scale: 0.95. This caused a split-second 'Pop' (1.0 -> 0.95) for every new entering card.
2. **Implementation**: Added explicit initial={{ scale: 0.95, opacity: 0.6, ... }} to the inner motion.div of CarouselItem.
3. **Outcome**: New cards entering the visuals now start at the correct dormant size (0.95) and do not 'pop' or flash. They only scale up if they happen to satisfy the Active/Preview condition (which is only the Leftmost card).
