Project: High-End Wedding Cinematography Agency Portfolio Tech Stack: React (Vite), Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion Role: Senior Creative Developer (Awwwards/Vogue style)

Goal: Build a single-page interactive portfolio. The aesthetic must be "Cinematic," "Emotional," and "Luxury." It should feel like a film production house website, not a standard wedding vendor site.

Core Dependencies:

framer-motion (Critical for the graceful, slow-motion feel)

react-intersection-observer

lucide-react

clsx, tailwind-merge

@fontsource/playfair-display (For elegant typography)

@fontsource/inter (For clean UI text)

Design System & Styling:

Background: bg-neutral-900 (Soft Deep Charcoal - softer than pure black).

Text: text-neutral-200 (Off-white).

Accent: text-rose-200 or text-amber-100 (Champagne/Gold tones).

Typography Mix:

Headings: Elegant Serif (Playfair Display) for Couple Names.

Subtitles: All-caps Sans-Serif (Inter) with wide tracking for "THE WEDDING FILM" labels.

KEY COMPONENT: The "Love Stories" Horizontal Slider

Interaction: A horizontal strip of cinematic wedding stills.

Scroll Logic:

Standard vertical scroll commands the horizontal movement.

Speed: Make the friction slightly higher so the movement feels "heavy" and "graceful" rather than fast and snappy.

The "Center Stage" Effect:

As an image enters the center viewport:

Scale: Increases to 1.3 (Zoom in on the emotion).

Opacity: 100%.

Filter: Full Color.

Side Images:

Scale: 0.9.

Opacity: 50%.

Filter: grayscale(100%) (This makes the center image pop dramatically).

Dynamic Overlay UI:

Location/Date: As the center image changes, update the text in the corner (e.g., "Udaipur, India" | "Dec 2024").

Floating "Play" Button: A subtle circular button that follows the cursor when hovering over the active center image, inviting the user to "Watch Trailer."

Services Section (Commercial vs. Wedding):

A section below the slider titled "OUR CRAFT."

Two distinct columns or tabs:

"Weddings" (Cinematography, Candid Photography, Pre-Wedding).

"Commercial" (Brand Stories, Editorials).

Data Structure (data.js):

Create a dataset with wedding-focused placeholders.

Fields: id, coupleName (e.g., "Aditi & Vihaan"), category (e.g., "The Royal Wedding"), image (Use Unsplash keywords: indian wedding, bride, groom, luxury event), location.

Output: Generate the App.jsx, data.js, and index.css. Focus heavily on the smoothness of the useTransform logic in Framer Motion.
